# Mission Control

Your Mission. Your Choices. Your Survival.

Mission Control is an interactive space mission simulation game designed to introduce students to the challenges of becoming a space explorer and establishing a sustainable outpost beyond Earth.

Players take on the role of a space scientist, make critical mission decisions, manage limited resources, explore different planetary environments, and experience how their choices influence mission success or failure.

Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

---

🌌 About the Project

Establishing a human presence beyond Earth requires careful planning, scientific understanding, and effective resource management.

Mission Control transforms these challenges into an engaging educational game where players experience the complexities of planning and managing a lunar or Martian mission.

Players can choose between two destinations:

- 🌕 Moon Mission: Explore the lunar environment, investigate the possibility of water ice near the lunar south pole, and plan a sustainable lunar base.
- 🔴 Mars Mission: Explore the Martian environment, investigate the planet's history, and search for signs of past or present life while planning a sustainable Mars base.

Each destination presents unique environmental challenges, discoveries, and decisions.

🎮 Gameplay

The game takes players through different stages of a space mission:

1. Mission Control – Prepare for launch, select your crew and equipment, and choose your destination.
2. Space Journey – Face unexpected events and make decisions that affect your mission resources.
3. Arrival & Exploration – Explore your selected destination and learn about its environment.
4. Base Establishment – Make decisions to support astronaut survival and build a sustainable outpost.
5. Mission Report – Review your performance, resource management, discoveries, and mission outcome.

✨ Key Features

- 🌍 Choice between Moon and Mars missions.
- 🚀 Interactive mission scenarios and decision-making.
- 🔋 Resource management involving fuel, oxygen, food, power, and crew morale.
- 🌞 Random space events and mission challenges.
- 🧩 Interactive mini-games and educational activities.
- 🔬 Space science facts and discoveries.
- 📊 Mission progress and final performance report.
- 🎨 Engaging and student-friendly interface.

🚀 Getting Started

Prerequisites

Make sure you have the following installed:

- Node.js
- npm
- Git

Installation

1. Clone the repository:
   
   git clone https://github.com/Smrithi-krishna/mission-control.git

2. Navigate to the project directory:
   
   cd mission-control

3. Install dependencies:
   
   npm install

4. Start the development server:
   
   npm run dev

5. Open the local URL displayed in your terminal.
